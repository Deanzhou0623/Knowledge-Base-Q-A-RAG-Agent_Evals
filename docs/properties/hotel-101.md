# Harbor Light Hotel

## Check-in Time

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM. The front desk is staffed until 11:00 PM.

## Pet Policy

Harbor Light Hotel permits one dog under 20 kilograms for a nightly fee of 25
Nova credits. Cats and other animals are not accepted unless they are service
animals.

## Cancellation Policy

Bookings may be cancelled without a fee through 2026-08-10 at 18:00 UTC. After
that deadline, the property charges the first night's rate.
